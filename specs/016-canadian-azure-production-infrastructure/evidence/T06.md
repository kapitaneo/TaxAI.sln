## [ASSUMED] decisions (Class B, for one-pass human override)

- Ledger immutability 90 days; upper bound 365 days.
- Ledger read freshness 15 minutes; attestation maximum age 4 hours (the
  regional objective).
- Canada East ledger account is LRS (no availability zones assumed in Canada
  East); Canada Central ledger is ZRS.
- Recovery profile accepts post-failover `Standard_LRS`/`Standard_ZRS` source
  redundancy until geo-redundancy is re-enabled.
- Ledger/evidence accounts use platform-managed encryption + infrastructure
  encryption (opaque identifiers only, like the key ring).
- A pristine database (no taxpayer data, session or activity) may bootstrap
  the ledger's genesis; firms/memberships provisioned beforehand do not count.
